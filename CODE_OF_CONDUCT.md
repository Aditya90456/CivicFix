# Code of Conduct

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community.

### Expected Behavior

* Be respectful and constructive.
* Welcome different ideas and perspectives.
* Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

### Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content.
* Sharing private information without permission.
* Deliberately disruptive or harmful behavior.

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers. Reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially.

By contributing to this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
